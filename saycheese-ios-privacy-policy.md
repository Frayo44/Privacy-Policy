This privacy policy explains how **SayCheese** and **SayCheese Watch Remote** for iPhone and Android, and the SayCheese apps for Garmin watches and Apple Watch (together, "the apps"), handle information. The apps are developed and operated by Yoav Franko ("I", "me"), who is responsible for the processing of personal information described here (the "controller"). Contact: [yoav.francoo@gmail.com](mailto:yoav.francoo@gmail.com).

By using the apps, you acknowledge this policy. If you don't agree with it, please don't use the apps.

**The short version**

*   Your photos and videos are taken and saved on your own devices. They are never uploaded to me.
*   There are no user accounts, and I don't sell your personal information.
*   The optional **Garmin HD preview** sends small live-preview images over the internet. They are kept in memory for at most 15 seconds and then deleted, and you can turn the feature off.
*   The apps use a few service providers for crash reports, usage statistics, ads (in the free version of SayCheese) and purchases. They are listed below.

**Information the apps handle**

**Photos, videos, camera and microphone.** The apps use your camera to take photos and videos, and your microphone to record video sound and, in SayCheese, for voice commands. Photos and videos are saved on your phone, or on the phone acting as the camera when you use two phones. I don't receive them.

**Connections between your devices.** When two phones, or a phone and a watch, work together, they communicate directly over Wi-Fi Direct, Bluetooth, your local network or Apple's watch connection. The live preview and the photos you take travel only between your own devices. On Garmin watches, these messages pass through Garmin's Garmin Connect app on your phone.

**Voice commands.** Voice commands in SayCheese are recognized on your phone by an offline speech model. Your voice is not recorded or sent anywhere. The model file is downloaded once from GitHub the first time you use voice commands.

**Garmin HD preview.** Garmin watches can only receive full-color pictures from the internet. While HD preview is on and your phone is online:

*   your phone sends small live-preview images, the size of your watch screen, to a relay server I operate on Cloudflare, over an encrypted (https) connection. Saved photos and videos are never sent this way;
*   the relay keeps only the newest image, in memory only. It is never written to disk, is deleted within 15 seconds, and the relay keeps no logs of these requests;
*   each session uses a random, unguessable address known only to your phone, your watch and Garmin's image service;
*   your watch fetches the images through Garmin's image service, which resizes them for your watch and is operated by Garmin under [Garmin's privacy policy](https://www.garmin.com/en-US/privacy/global/).

You can turn HD preview off at any time under **Settings > HD preview**, on the phone or on the watch. Without it, or without internet, the preview uses Bluetooth only.

**Crash reports.** If an app crashes or hits an error, Firebase Crashlytics collects technical information such as your device model, operating system version, app version, the app's settings at the time, the time of the error and a pseudonymous identifier for your app installation. Your IP address is processed by Google to deliver the report.

**Usage statistics.** Google Analytics for Firebase collects pseudonymous usage data, such as which features are used, together with an app-installation identifier, device and operating system information and an approximate location (country or region) derived from your IP address. It does not include your name, email or photos.

**Advertising (free version of SayCheese).** Ads are provided by Google AdMob, which may collect device information, your IP address, ad interactions and, on Android, your device's advertising ID, and may use them to show and measure ads. On iPhone, the apps don't ask for permission to track you across other companies' apps, so Apple's advertising identifier is not available to them. Premium users don't see ads.

**Purchases.** In-app purchases in SayCheese are processed by Apple or Google. RevenueCat helps me manage them using an anonymous app user identifier and your purchase and subscription records. I never receive your payment card details.

**Settings and support.** Your app settings are stored on your device. If you email me, I use your email address and message only to reply.

I use the information above only to provide and improve the apps:

*   to provide features you ask for, such as the live preview, HD preview and purchases (performance of a contract);
*   to keep the apps working, secure and free of bugs, and to understand which features are used (my legitimate interests in running and improving the apps);
*   to show ads in the free version of SayCheese (my legitimate interest in funding the free version, and your consent where the law requires it);
*   to comply with legal obligations and enforce my rights, where needed.

**International transfers**

These providers may process information on servers outside your country, including in the United States. Where required, they use recognized safeguards for such transfers, such as the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses.

**How long information is kept**

*   HD preview images: in memory only, deleted within 15 seconds.
*   Crash reports: kept by Firebase Crashlytics for about 90 days.
*   Usage statistics: kept under Google Analytics' retention settings, then deleted or aggregated.
*   Purchase records: kept by Apple, Google and RevenueCat as long as needed to provide your purchases and as required by law.
*   Support emails: as long as needed to handle your request.
